The board every hazard, its rate, its mechanism, and where the number came from

Rates are per year. observed means a directly counted frequency, geological a recurrence read off the rock or ice record, modelled a physical calculation, elicited an expert or forecaster survey, and estimated a figure this project had to construct because no published one exists. The last of those is the one to be suspicious of.

Against the published estimates is this model saying something the field does not?

Deep time the ends that are not risks but schedules

Everything above is a dice roll. Everything below is a calendar. If humanity gets through the hazardous part, these are what is left — and the first of them is not the Sun going out, but the Sun getting brighter.
